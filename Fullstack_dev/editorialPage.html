ANIME

Anime is a style of hand-drawn or computer animation that originated in and is widely produced in Japan. It features vibrant characters, distinct artwork (often including large eyes and dynamic hair), and complex, serialized storytelling that spans every genre from romance to sci-fi.

Anime is a distinct style of hand-drawn and computer animation originating in Japan. It features vibrant art, large expressive eyes, dynamic characters, and diverse storylines spanning all genres, from children's shows to complex, mature or action-packed plots.

Anime is a style of Japanese animated film and television production. Outside of Japan, the term specifically refers to animation created in Japan, known for vibrant characters, distinct visual traits like large eyes, and deep serialized storytelling. In Japan, however, the word describes all forms of animation.
